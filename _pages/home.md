I am a data engineer with a research background in evolutionary computation. My master's research at Wuhan University of Science and Technology focused on **constrained multi-objective optimization** and resulted in a first-author article in *Information Sciences* (2024). Since July 2024 I have worked in industry on **data platforms and internal AI applications**: migrating a traditional data warehouse to a lakehouse architecture and building a semantic-layer data agent for natural-language data analysis.

I am interested in PhD research where optimization meets data-driven and AI methods, and in engineering roles in AI data engineering and data platforms.

## Selected projects

**[Lakehouse Migration]({{ '/projects/lakehouse-migration/' | relative_url }})**  
Migrating a Kettle/DataX/MySQL data warehouse to a Flink, Apache Paimon and StarRocks lakehouse with dependency-driven scheduling. The daily warehouse update time dropped from over 4 hours to about 2 hours.

**[Light-ChatBI: A Semantic-Layer Data Agent]({{ '/projects/light-chatbi-data-agent/' | relative_url }})**  
An internal natural-language data analysis assistant in which the language model selects metrics and dimensions while SQL is generated deterministically from a semantic layer.
